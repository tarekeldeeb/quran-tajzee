## 2. Where a rubʿ may start

Every hizb starts at a phrase start (a new subject). A rubʿ may also start at another aya end inside a phrase, as the traditional rubʿ do, at a penalty m: such a start costs as much as a rubʿ m off its target. Without a cap:

With phrase starts only, the limit is phrase granularity: a juz has about 36 phrases, 4.6 per rubʿ, and a phrase of 10 long ayat is a fifth of a rubʿ. The default, m = 10.0% with a cap of ±10.0%, starts 58 rubʿ inside a phrase (traditional: 79 of the 210 inner rubʿ starts fall inside one of our phrases). Each is listed in `data/rub/phrase-splits.tsv` as a proposed phrase split for the next review: if the split is accepted, the rubʿ opens a phrase; if not, re-solve with the phrase kept whole.

## 5. Traditional juz starts inside a phrase

The juz are fixed, so where a traditional juz start falls inside a Task 1 phrase, that phrase is split there (11 cases): 3:93, 4:24, 6:111, 7:88, 9:93, 11:6, 12:53, 18:75, 27:56, 29:46, 33:31. These are listed with reason `juz` in `data/rub/phrase-splits.tsv` for the phrase review.

## 6. Takeaways

- The traditional juz are within ±6%, but their hizb reach ±14% and their rubʿ ±35% of their share of the juz: the imbalance is in the finer divisions.
- Keeping the juz and re-cutting inside them brings hizb to ±3.7% and rubʿ to ±10%, with every hizb opening a subject.
- How far rubʿ can be balanced depends on how fine the phrases are; the proposed splits are where a finer phrase would help most.
