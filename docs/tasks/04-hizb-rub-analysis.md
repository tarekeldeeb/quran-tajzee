## 2. Methods and settings

The default starts every hizb and rubʿ at a phrase start, and minimises the worst unit, then the sum of squares. The penalized rows let a rubʿ start inside a phrase, at a penalty m: such a start costs as much as a rubʿ m off its target. The cap is a hard limit on every rubʿ. In all rows but the last, a hizb must start at a phrase start.

| variant | worst hizb | hizb CV | worst rubʿ | rubʿ CV | rubʿ inside a phrase | of which hizb |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **default**: nested, phrase starts only, minimax + least squares | ±3.6% | 1.4% | ±20.8% | 6.5% | 0 | 0 |
| joint (8 rubʿ per juz at once), phrase starts only | ±6.0% | 2.2% | ±20.8% | 5.9% | 0 | 0 |
| nested, phrase starts only, least squares | ±3.6% | 1.4% | ±20.8% | 6.4% | 0 | 0 |
| penalized: mid-phrase rubʿ at m 15% | ±3.6% | 1.4% | ±20.8% | 5.4% | 8 | 0 |
| penalized: m 10%, cap ±10% | ±3.6% | 1.4% | ±20.8% | 4.6% | 23 | 0 |
| penalized: m 5% | ±3.6% | 1.4% | ±20.8% | 3.6% | 66 | 0 |
| any aya end, hizb too (m 0) | ±1.0% | 0.4% | ±14.5% | 2.8% | 119 | 17 |


The hizb are the same in every nested row. Their worst case, ±3.6%, is set by phrase granularity in one juz. For the rubʿ, phrase granularity is the limit: a hizb holds about 18 phrases, 4.6 per rubʿ, and one long phrase can be a fifth of a rubʿ. Each phrase split accepted in review (section 6) moves the default towards the penalized rows without ever cutting a phrase.

## 5. Boundaries that cut into a phrase

A boundary cuts into a phrase when it starts a unit in the middle of a Task 1 subject phrase. The next aya then continues the subject of the last aya of the previous unit. Each unit is counted at its own level: a juz start is also a hizb and rubʿ start, but it is counted once, as a juz.

| boundary | traditional: inside a phrase | median depth into the phrase | new |
|---|---|---|---|
| juz starts | 11 of 30 (37%) | 38% | kept (traditional) |
| second-hizb starts | 12 of 30 (40%) | 60% | 0 |
| other rubʿ starts | 67 of 180 (37%) | 50% | 0 |
| all 240 | 90 of 240 (38%) | 50% | 0 beyond the 11 juz |


The traditional hizb and rubʿ cut into a phrase 79 times out of 210. **The new hizb and rubʿ never do:** every one of them opens a phrase. The only phrases cut are by the 11 traditional juz starts that are kept as they are. Splitting those phrases in review (next section) removes even those. Depth is how far into the phrase the boundary falls, as a share of the phrase's ayat. Every case is listed in `data/rub/traditional-phrase-cuts.tsv`.
