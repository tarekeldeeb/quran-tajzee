- `penalized` (default): least squares plus the surah-break penalty (10% on the last 5 juz, 3% on the others), cap ±5%.
- `minimax`: the smallest possible worst juz on the mix; the other juz are left loose.
- `lsq`: the smallest sum of squared deviations on the mix.
- `minimax+lsq`: least squares among the divisions that reach the minimax worst case.
- `each`: no mixing; the smallest worst deviation of *either* score in any juz, then least squares.
